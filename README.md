## Overview

This assignment is for **Mathematics for Computing** (Semester 1, MTech CSE – AI & Software Engineering, CUSAT), built as a LaTeX Beamer presentation.

It covers two parts:

**1. Foundational Concepts**
- Definition of Statistics
- Functions and activities of the Department of Statistics, Kerala and India
- Data, Information, Knowledge, Intelligence (DIKW) with examples
- Definition of Artificial Intelligence
- Types of data: Univariate, Bivariate, Multivariate
- Data representation methods (tables, bar graphs, histograms, pie charts, line graphs)
- Measures of Central Tendency (Mean, Median, Mode)
- Measures of Dispersion (Range, Variance, Standard Deviation)

**2. Module 3 – Descriptive & Frequentist Statistics**
- Descriptive Statistics: Histogram, Sample mean & variance, Order statistics, Sample covariance, Sample covariance matrix
- Frequentist Statistics: IID sampling, Mean square error, Consistency, Confidence intervals, Parametric & Non-parametric model estimation

Each topic is presented with a definition, the relevant formula, and a key point to remember — built for quick exam reference. Reference: *Probability and Statistics for Data Science* (Carlos Fernandez-Granda).
